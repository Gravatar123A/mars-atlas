# NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge alignment

Reviewed 2026-11-15 against the [full official Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map statement](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/interplanetary-survival-guide-martian-map/), last published by Space Apps on 2026-11-13. The challenge asks for a Mars surface map, tool, or interface using NASA data to support a human exploration mission. Its examples and potential considerations are suggestions, not mandatory acceptance criteria.

| Official direction | MARS//ATLAS implementation | Evidence and remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrated, local view of a location or route using multiple NASA missions | Jezero, Gale, and Gusev region selector with MRO CTX and HiRISE, Mars Odyssey THEMIS, and MGS MOLA visual layers; source inspector separates imagery from numerical elevation. Gale adds a USGS merged elevation product with MRO, Mars Express, and MOLA inputs. | [NASA Mars Trek catalog](https://trek.nasa.gov/tiles/apidoc/trekAPI.html?body=mars). Imagery requests depend on the live service. Gale source provenance includes ESA Mars Express and is labelled accordingly. |
| Consider at least three kinds of data | Optical imagery, thermal infrared imagery, and topographic elevation are available. | THEMIS is a historical visual mosaic, not current temperature. Topography is numerical USGS/JPL CTX DEM. No spectral, radiation, chemical, or live atmospheric layer is claimed. |
| Help a crew navigate while avoiding significant obstacles | DEM-based A* route profiles, optional waypoint, hard model slope limit, ascent, travel estimate, slope-gradient display, steepest-edge inspection, and simplified bearing/turn checkpoints. Jezero selects a 20 m landing DEM or an 80 m sampled regional DEM. Gale uses a 20 m sampled landing-area DEM. Gusev has no route DEM and route controls are disabled. | [USGS Jezero landing terrain](https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/mars2020_trn/CTX/index.html), [USGS/JPL Jezero regional terrain](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_science_investigation_ctx_dem_mosaic), [USGS Gale terrain](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_msl_gale_merged_dem_1m). Small rocks, soil and hazards between cells are unknown. No operational safety certification. |
| Connect the route to science goals | Documented landing and Perseverance Sol 426 / Sol 464 positions, delta-front science context, waypoint itinerary, cited export brief; Gale includes documented Bradbury Landing and a clearly labelled scenario destination; Gusev includes documented Spirit landing and Columbia Hills context. | NASA records linked in [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md). Historical rover coordinates are context points, not approved EVA science sites or mapped outcrop boundaries. The Gale destination is a user scenario coordinate. |
| Consider current conditions and field context | The interface explicitly distinguishes historical imagery and model assumptions from current observations. | Live weather, radiation, daylight and communications are not implemented; the official statement lists such considerations as possibilities. NASA rover point-of-view imagery is linked through the challenge resources but not yet located against route stops in this app. |

The official [Resources tab](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/interplanetary-survival-guide-martian-map/) lists Mars Trek, the USGS-NASA Planetary Geologic Mapping Program, a Curiosity route example, NASA Worldview, and the Perseverance raw image gallery. The app uses Mars Trek and CTX products directly; NASA Worldview is an Earth service and is not presented as Mars data.
